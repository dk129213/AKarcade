03 The machine

Cabinet 01 is a tribute to the upright machines that lined arcades in the '80s: a backlit marquee, a curved CRT behind a black bezel, a ball-top joystick and a coin door that ate your pocket money. Inside, a real NES emulator runs genuine 8-bit game cartridges, the same code that runs on the original console.
